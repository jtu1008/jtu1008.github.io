<span style="font-size:1em">**Primary Work: Language Reasoning and Understanding through Dense Paraphrasing**</span>

Language reasoning and understanding is a fundamental task in NLP.
We, as humans, interpret sentences as contextualized components of a narrative or discourse,
by both filling in missing information, and reasoning about event consequences.
My work focuses on _Dense Paraphrasing (DP)_, a linguistically-motivated generic strategy for the _enrichment of the expression_ through both its lexical semantics
and its dynamic contribution to the text in the whole narrative.
We apply DP to advance many downstream tasks, including _question answering_, _coreference resolution_, and _event tracking_.
