Hi — I'm Jingxuan. I build LLM and agentic systems for medical coding at CodaMetrix, and I
did my Ph.D. in computational linguistics before that. This is where I'll write **learning
notes** — plain-language explainers on AI, NLP, and the things I'm figuring out as I go.

First up: a short series on **medical coding and how AI fits into it**, written from the
perspective of someone learning the domain themselves.

## What you can expect

Posts here aim to be approachable and concrete — lots of examples, the occasional diagram,
and a clear takeaway. As a quick demo, here's the kind of flowchart I'll use to explain how
a doctor's visit becomes a bill:
